### What changed and why

The rendered videos read softer, duller, and "not my design" against the Claude Design HTML — but the palette was already identical (ΔE ≈ 0). The gap was three things: a **flat 1080p raster** (no supersampling), a pipeline that **re-derived every token one notch heavier/rounder/softer** (800 vs 700, 14px vs 4px, a resting shadow on every card, springs instead of eased tweens), and a **silent serif fallback** in the sizzle (beats had no font set → browser-default Times).

This branch fixes all three across six phases, then locks them so they can't silently drift back. Two intended identities are now correct: **lessons = Syne 700**, **marketing/sizzle = Playfair headlines · Syne chrome · mono captions**.
