Topic 1: Getting Started with AI Assistants
~27 min of lessons
Learning Outcomes
Choose: match an assistant to the job in front of you.
Prompt: ask clearly for what you need.
Check: review an answer before you rely on it.
Personalize: set an assistant up once so it remembers how you work.
Lesson 1.1: How This Course Works (3 min)
Time
Content
1 min
Welcome + How the Course Is Paced
Short lessons, one skill each. Every skill is shown in an assistant first, then practiced on the learner's own work.
1 min
Topic 1 at a Glance + the Lesson Rhythm
The five Topic 1 lessons as a map with 1.1 active, and the rhythm every lesson repeats: see the idea, watch it in a tool, try it on your own task.
1 min
Get Ready to Practice
A four-item setup checklist. Engagement moment: pick one real task from your week and hold onto it. Hand-off to Lesson 1.2.
Lesson 1.2: Picking the Right Assistant (6 min)
Time
Content
2 min
Side-by-Side Answers
One everyday request answered by three assistants, to show that style differs while the fundamentals stay the same.
2 min
The Assistants in This Course
A short card for each assistant profile used in the sample lessons.
2 min
Match the Tool to the Job
A rule of thumb: pick one assistant for writing and one for research, then stick with them for a week.
Lesson 1.3: Writing Clear Prompts (7 min)
Time
Content
2 min
Why Vague Prompts Disappoint
A vague request next to a specific one, with the difference in the answers called out.
3 min
The Three-Question Prompt Check
Before sending a prompt, answer three questions: who is it for, what do you need, and what does done look like.
2 min
Rewrite a Prompt Together
Learners rewrite a sample prompt using the three questions while the instructor narrates.

Time
Content
2 min

Why a confident, well-written answer can still be wrong, shown with an invented example.
2 min
A Five-Point Review Checklist
Facts, numbers, names, tone, and anything missing.
2 min
Spot the Problem
A sample answer with two planted errors for learners to find.

Time
Content

Settings Walkthrough

1 min
Topic 1 Wrap-Up
What learners can now do, and a pointer to the next topic.

Topic 3: Automating Everyday Work
~6 min of lessons
Learning Outcomes
Map: turn one repetitive task into inputs, steps, and an output.
Lesson 3.1: Mapping a Repetitive Task (6 min)
Time
Content
2 min
Find the Task Worth Automating
Look for work that is frequent, predictable, and low risk.
2 min
Break It Into Steps
Write the task down as inputs, a short numbered list of steps, and a single output.
2 min
Decide What Stays Human
Mark the steps that need judgement and keep those with the learner.
